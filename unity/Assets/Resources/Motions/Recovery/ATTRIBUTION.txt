Motion data used in this game was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
Source: CMU subject 140, trial 03, Get Up Laying on Side (120 Hz).
BVH conversion: Bruce Hahne, 2010; mirror: github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap.

CMU permits research and commercial product use, but prohibits direct resale
of the data, including converted data. See https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/ .
The BVH converter adds no additional use restrictions.
These files are retargeted game animation assets with costume-specific
contact correction. They are not offered as a standalone motion dataset.
The source capture does not contain real finger motion.

Character models retain their existing separate attribution and permissions.
See each Resources/Characters/<hero>/ attribution and docs/美术与音频说明.md.
