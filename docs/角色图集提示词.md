# 角色动作图集提示词

使用内置 `image_gen`，未使用 CLI/API fallback。摄像头画面不参与生成。

## 迪迦最终图集

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production sprite atlas for a Unity family Ultraman fighting game.
Create an EXACT 4-column by 2-row sprite sheet, eight equal rectangular cells, ideally 2048x1536 pixels. Each cell has ONE full-body Ultraman Tiga MULTI TYPE, faithful to the original Japanese television series character design. The hero must be immediately recognizable as original Ultraman Tiga, not a generic robot. Sharp iconic silver helmet and central crest, almond luminous white eyes, blue forehead crystal, gold outlined layered chest collar and small cyan chest timer, correct asymmetric-looking red/purple/silver suit panels. Adult athletic proportions, sophisticated tokusatsu anime game illustration with smooth metallic highlights and detailed shading, no chibi.
All eight figures at identical scale, complete head and feet, same camera at slight 3/4 front angle facing SCREEN RIGHT toward an opponent. Every cell: feet resting on a baseline 94% down the cell, hips centered at 48% cell width, head around 15% down except raised arms, 6% clear margins. Do not overlap neighboring cells. A perfectly flat saturated pure GREEN chroma background #00FF00 everywhere outside character. NO CHECKERBOARD! The green screen is intentional for the game shader. No transparency simulation, no ground, no shadows, no grid, no cell borders, no text, no numbers, no glow effects outside body, no beam or shield objects (game adds those).
Read order left to right top to bottom:
1. Neutral classic combat guard, bent knees, fists ready.
2. Punch anticipation, right fist pulled back and torso twisting slightly.
3. Full extended straight punch toward screen right, feet planted wide.
4. Defense, both forearms held together crossed in front of upper chest.
5. Tiga's authentic Zeperion beam L-arm pose, right forearm upright and left forearm horizontal across chest. Hands visible and correct. No emitted beam.
6. Gentle hit reaction, leaning back slightly, arms open, still standing.
7. Transformation, both arms stretched high above head in a V, feet firmly down.
8. Victorious confident classic hero pose, one fist raised high and other on hip.
This is one coherent production atlas, same Tiga design and consistent rendering in all eight cells.
```

## 哥尔赞底色处理

输入为上一步图集，使用内置 image_gen 编辑。

```text
Use case: background-extraction. Edit the provided eight-pose Golza game sprite atlas. Keep the eight poses, recognizable Golza identity, stony texture and original character colors. Exact 4 equal columns x 2 equal rows grid. Fix sprites crossing their cells: ensure each entire character and its tail, claws, feet sits within its own cell with at least 5% cell-width clear margin on BOTH left and right; especially the swipe pose row1 column3 and recoil row2 column3. Do not let any hand enter an adjacent cell. Keep feet at 94% down each cell, identical scale across poses. Replace EVERY gray/white checkerboard background pixel with a perfectly flat pure chroma green RGB(0,255,0) #00FF00 background, including holes between limbs and tail. No checkerboard, shadows, lines, labels, borders or text. Crisp smooth cutout edges without green spill. A solid chroma green backdrop is required for runtime chroma-key rendering in Unity. Canvas retains 4:3 aspect and all eight full figures.
```

## 哥尔赞动作间距

输入为上一步绿色底图集，使用内置 image_gen 编辑。

```text
Re-layout this Golza eight-pose green-screen game atlas for production. The current poses are too close and cross cell boundaries. MUST SHRINK EACH OF THE EIGHT FIGURES TO 70% OF ITS CURRENT SIZE, NOT THE CANVAS. Keep each figure in the same grid cell and the same pose, same textures, same design. Canvas 1448x1086 or exact same 4:3 aspect, with 4 columns and 2 rows. EACH CELL has huge pure green EMPTY padding on all four sides: at least 15% of the cell width at left and right, at least 15% cell height at top, feet baseline at 85% of cell height. Align the center of each character to center of its own cell. Every complete figure must remain fully isolated WITH ITS TAIL AND ALL CLAWS inside its cell; no hand, tail or foot may touch cell edges. Maintain 4 columns x 2 rows, 8 complete figures. Solid #00FF00 green background with no text, no grid lines, no checkerboard, no shadows. This edit changes character SCALE AND POSITION for safe slicing; keep character artwork otherwise unchanged.
```

## 迪迦动作间距

```text
Re-layout this eight-pose Ultraman Tiga green-screen game atlas for production. Current bottom-row raised hands cross into top-row cells. MUST SHRINK EACH OF THE EIGHT FIGURES TO 70% OF ITS CURRENT SIZE, NOT THE CANVAS. Keep each figure in the same grid cell and the same pose, identity, silver helmet, original suit colors and chest details. Canvas 1448x1086 or exact same 4:3 aspect, 4 columns and 2 rows. EACH CELL has large pure green EMPTY padding: at least 15% cell width left and right, at least 15% cell height top, feet baseline at 85% cell height. Align each character centered inside its own cell. ALL raised fists in row 2 MUST BE ENTIRELY BELOW the horizontal midline, with green space above them. Entire figure, all fingers, helmet, boots must be inside own cell. No body parts cross cell borders. Maintain exact same relative anatomical scale across all poses, even raised-arm poses. Solid #00FF00 green backdrop, no grid, labels, shadows or checkerboard. Edit character SCALE AND POSITION to make safe slicing possible, preserve the character art otherwise.
```

首轮输出为 RGB 棋盘格背景，因此没有把它当作透明 PNG 使用；最终使用绿色底图集，通过 Unity shader 在显示时消除背景。
